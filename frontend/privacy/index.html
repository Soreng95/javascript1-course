Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 September 2026

1. Who we are

Rainy Days runs this store and decides how the personal data described below is handled.

2. What we collect

When you place an order we collect the contact, delivery and payment details you type into the checkout form, together with the items in your order.

We do not collect anything while you are only browsing.

3. How we use it

We use your details to process and deliver your order, to handle returns, and to answer you if you contact us. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Storage in your browser

Your shopping cart is kept in your own browser using local storage so it survives when you move between pages. It stays on your device, we cannot read it, and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings or by emptying the cart.

The store sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Sharing

We share delivery details with the carrier that brings your parcel, and payment details with the payment provider that processes the transaction. We share nothing else unless the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as accounting rules require, then deleted. Anything we no longer need is removed sooner.

7. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to privacy@rainydays.example and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is published on this page with a new date at the top.
